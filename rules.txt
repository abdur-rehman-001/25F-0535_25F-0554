 THE LOCKER HEIST - RULES

1. The detective starts with 100 credibility.
2. The game allows a maximum of 25 turns.
3. Search the seven locations to collect Word 1 letters.
4. Interview the eight people to collect Word 2 letters.
5. A wrong word reduces credibility by 10.
6. Some people need a minimum credibility before they can be interviewed.
7. Solve both words before accusing a suspect.
8. The score is saved in scoreBoard.txt after the game ends.
